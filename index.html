How to play

Open the popup

Click the 2048 icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the board.

Slide the tiles

Use the arrow keys, WASD, or a swipe to move every tile at once.

Merge matching numbers

Two tiles with the same number combine into one tile worth double.

Reach 2048

Keep merging to hit 2048 — then keep playing to push your score higher.
